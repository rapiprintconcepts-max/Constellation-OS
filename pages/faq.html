Frequently Asked Questions

What is Constellation OS?

Constellation OS is a unified operating system designed to streamline and connect every essential part of a manufacturing business — from quoting and scheduling to production, scanning, financial automation, and customer management.

Who is Constellation OS for?

It’s built for micro‑manufacturing studios, production facilities, R&D labs, hybrid operations, and multi‑department teams.

Does Constellation OS replace my existing tools?

No. Constellation OS connects and unifies the tools you already use. It reduces fragmentation and manual work without forcing you to change platforms.

Is Constellation OS scalable?

Yes. It works for single‑operator studios all the way up to multi‑department manufacturing facilities.

Does Constellation OS support scanning workflows?

Yes. Scanning and reconstruction workflows are integrated directly into the system for precision and consistency.
