About Constellation OS

The unified operating system built for modern manufacturing.

Our Mission

Constellation OS exists to unify manufacturing operations of every size — from micro‑studios to full production facilities. We believe that clarity, speed, and predictability should be accessible to every shop, not just enterprise‑scale factories.

Why We Built Constellation OS

Manufacturing businesses rely on dozens of disconnected tools, spreadsheets, machines, and workflows. Constellation OS brings all of these elements together into one seamless system — reducing friction, eliminating fragmentation, and giving operators and owners complete visibility into their operation.

Rapiprint Design Works LLC

Constellation OS is developed by Rapiprint Design Works LLC, a micro‑manufacturing and R&D studio based in Iowa. Our experience across prototyping, production, scanning, and workflow engineering drives every feature of the platform.

Our Background

With years of hands‑on experience in manufacturing environments, we understand the challenges shops face: scheduling chaos, quoting inconsistencies, machine downtime, workflow fragmentation, and financial blind spots.

Our Approach

We build systems that are predictable, modular, and architecture‑driven — ensuring that every part of your operation stays connected, synchronized, and easy to manage.

What’s Next

Constellation OS continues to evolve with new modules, deeper machine integration, expanded scanning workflows, and advanced UFC financial intelligence. Our roadmap is built around real manufacturing needs — not trends.
